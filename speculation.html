KEEPING THE SITE TRUSTWORTHY

How This Page Works

1

Rockstar confirmation stays on the main site.

If Rockstar explicitly announces something, it belongs in the confirmed database and can graduate out of speculation.

2

Creator analysis is attributed to the creator.

Videos are presented as opinions, breakdowns, reactions, theories, or rumors. An embedded creator title does not become a factual claim by this site.

3

Leaks and rumors get obvious labels.

No rumor should quietly appear in Story, Map, Vehicles, Music, or Online as though Rockstar confirmed it.

4

The creator lineup can stay fresh.

As new GTA VI analysis appears, older embeds can rotate out while especially useful evergreen breakdowns remain available.
