THE MAIN STORY

Jason + Lucia

Rockstar’s confirmed premise, separated from guesses and leaks.

Confirmed

The setup

Jason and Lucia know the odds are stacked against them. After an easy score goes wrong, they are pulled into a criminal conspiracy stretching across Leonida and have to depend on each other to survive.

Official Rockstar source ↗
Confirmed

Lucia’s starting point

Lucia has been held in Leonida Penitentiary after fighting for her family. Rockstar says luck helped her get out, and she is determined to make smarter moves from here.

Confirmed

Jason’s background

Jason grew up around grifters and crooks, served in the Army, then ended up in the Keys working for local drug runners.

Not announced

Mission list

PEOPLE OF LEONIDA

Confirmed Characters

Eight official profile entries cover the central pair and major supporting players Rockstar has introduced.

Confirmed

Jason Duval

Army veteran turned Keys-connected criminal associate. Jason wants an easier life, but his path with Lucia keeps getting more complicated.

Confirmed

Lucia Caminos

Fresh out of Leonida Penitentiary, determined to improve the odds for herself and her family after years of hard lessons.

Confirmed

Cal Hampton

Jason’s friend and fellow Brian associate. He is happiest at home, following conspiracies and listening to Coast Guard communications.

Confirmed

Boobie Ike

A Vice City legend with interests in real estate, a strip club, and a recording studio. His biggest focus is Only Raw Records.

Confirmed

Dre’Quan Priest

A hustler focused on making it in music. He is building Only Raw Records and has signed Real Dimez.

Confirmed

Real Dimez

Bae-Luxe and Roxy, longtime friends turned rap duo who combine music, social media, and street savvy.

Confirmed

Raul Bautista

A seasoned bank robber who is always looking for talented people willing to take bigger risks for bigger rewards.

Confirmed

Brian Heder

Veteran Keys smuggler running product through his boat yard. Jason stays at one of Brian’s properties in exchange for helping with local shakedowns.
